A SMOOTHER JOURNEY

A few things worth knowing.

01 / ARRIVE & DEPART

Fly in south. Fly out north.

Arrive in Kagoshima, potentially via a domestic connection. Depart from New Chitose Airport near Sapporo. Leave time for airport transfers on days 1, 14, and 18.

02 / TRAVEL LIGHT

Seven bases, not eighteen.

Stay in Kyoto for Osaka and Nara day trips. Consider forwarding large bags from Kyoto to Tokyo and carrying a smaller bag on transfer days.

03 / TICKETS & TIMING

A rail pass isn't automatic.

Compare individual tickets with passes, accounting for the Tokyo-Hakodate flight. Journey times here are approximate, not live schedules; confirm services and bookings before travel.

04 / FOLLOW THE SEASON

Leave room for the weather.

No travel dates assumed. Southern Japan can be very hot in summer; Hokkaido needs weather margin in winter. Keep volcano visits, hikes, and night views flexible.
