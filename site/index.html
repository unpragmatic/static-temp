A SMOOTHER JOURNEY

A few things worth knowing.

01 / ARRIVE & DEPART

The anchors are shared.

Land in Kagoshima November 18 at 15:00. All routes fly New Chitose-Haneda December 4 and depart internationally from Tokyo December 7, leaving three Tokyo nights between flights. Confirm the final airport and departure time; December 7 timings stay relative until those are known.

02 / BOOKING PRIORITIES

The holiday weekend matters.

November 23 is Labor Thanksgiving Day. Routes A and C transfer Hiroshima-Kyoto that day, staying November 23-27. Route B reaches Kyoto November 25 and leaves November 30. All are in foliage season. Confirm rooms, seats, flights and timed attractions; no availability is assumed.

03 / PACK & TRANSFER

Two seasons in one bag.

Bring layers, a warm windproof coat, gloves and grippy waterproof shoes. Stay in Kyoto for Osaka and Nara. Allow 30-60 minutes of hotel-to-station access before the suggested rail target, and aim to reach domestic terminals at least 90 minutes before the booked flight, or earlier if required.

04 / DAYLIGHT & WEATHER

Winter-ready, not snow-promised.

Hokkaido in late November and early December means possible ice and sunset around 4 pm. Finish exposed walking before dusk and keep indoor alternatives. No guaranteed snow, ski opening, illumination dates or foliage peak. All schedule times are JST; check forecasts and service alerts each day.

Check before booking: Kagoshima Airport access / JNTO November guide / JMA warnings / JR East / JR Hokkaido. These are planning references, not verification of the dated services. No live fares, transport departure times, opening hours or availability are confirmed.
