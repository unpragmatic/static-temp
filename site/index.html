A SMOOTHER JOURNEY

A few things worth knowing.

01 / ARRIVE & DEPART

Fly in south. Finish in Tokyo.

Arrive in Kagoshima on November 17. Fly Haneda-Hakodate November 28 and New Chitose-Haneda December 3. Depart internationally from Tokyo December 6; confirm Haneda versus Narita. The return to Tokyo separates the Hokkaido flight from departure by three nights.

02 / BOOKING PRIORITIES

The holiday weekend matters.

Reserve Kyoto's four nights (November 22-26) and the November 22 shinkansen first. November 23 is Labor Thanksgiving Day. Osaka moves to that holiday; Arashiyama moves to November 25, still a busy foliage-season day. Book flights, key hotels, and timed-entry attractions now.

03 / PACK & TRANSFER

Two seasons in one bag.

Bring layers, a warm windproof outer layer, gloves, and grippy waterproof shoes. Keep Kyoto as the base for Osaka and Nara. Ask your first Tokyo hotel about storing a bag until your return; do not assume storage is available. Compare rail tickets with passes, excluding both domestic flights.

04 / DAYLIGHT & WEATHER

Winter-ready, not snow-promised.

Hokkaido on November 28-December 2 means early winter, possible ice, and sunset around 4 pm. Prioritize outdoor visits before dusk. No guaranteed snow, ski opening, illumination dates, or foliage peak. December 2 is a flexible city day; check forecasts, opening hours, and transport alerts daily.
